## 图 1　中国地方鸡种重测序采样个体数的省级分布

> **图 1　中国地方鸡种重测序采样个体数的省级分布。** 按省汇总本研究所采纳的重测序个体数（共 363 只，来自 24 条品种记录、10 个省份）。填色分级为 1–19、20–49 与 ≥50 只，浅灰色表示该省无采纳样本。底图含国界、省界、南海诸岛及九段线；图上不标注地名，省份与品种的对应关系见正文表 X。数据取自 label1029.xlsx 中经蓝色标注（FF0070C0）或注明为本实验室数据的记录，重复上传的 2 条记录已剔除。

**English**

> **Figure 1. Provincial distribution of the adopted WGS samples of Chinese indigenous chicken breeds.** Adopted individuals are summed per province (363 individuals from 24 breed records across 10 provinces). Fills are classed as 1–19, 20–49 and ≥50 individuals; light grey marks provinces without adopted samples. Basemap: national border, provincial boundaries, South China Sea islands and the nine-dash line; no place names are printed on the map, and the province↔breed lookup is given in Table X. Records were taken from `label1029.xlsx` where the row is shaded blue (FF0070C0) or tagged as in-house data; two duplicate re-uploads were removed.

## 图 2　中国地方鸡种重测序采样点的地理分布

> **图 2　中国地方鸡种重测序采样点的地理分布。** 每个圆点代表一个采样点，共 21 个；圆点面积与颜色共同编码该点采纳的个体数（1–9、10–19、20–29、≥30 只），双重编码可保证黑白打印时仍能区分等级。虚线圆表示该记录仅精确到州或平原级，点位取该产区的代表点；其余点位取县或市政府驻地坐标。左下角插图为黔西—湘西地区（103.4–111.9°E，23.6–29.2°N）的放大显示，对应范围已在主图中以方框标出。底图与数据来源同图 1。

**English**

> **Figure 2. Geographic distribution of the sampling sites.** Each circle is one sampling site (21 in total). Circle area and colour jointly encode the number of adopted individuals at that site (1–9, 10–19, 20–29, ≥30 individuals), so the classes stay distinguishable in greyscale print. Dashed circles mark records resolved only to prefecture or plain level, placed at the representative point of that production area; all other sites are plotted at the county or city government seat. The lower-left inset enlarges southwest China (103.4–111.9°E, 23.6–29.2°N), boxed on the main map. Basemap and data source as in Figure 1.
