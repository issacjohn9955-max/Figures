# Suggested legend (edit for the manuscript)

Genome-wide distribution of reference transposable elements and assembly-derived variation on chicken GRCg7b. From outside inward: reference LINE, LTR, SINE and DNA-transposon union coverage (%), SNV discovery density, Jasmine SV density and extended TE/repeat-variation (TEV) density. All tracks use the same1Mb genomic windows; terminal windows use their actual lengths. The four TE tracks use independent display axes (32%,8.2%,0.23%,15%); modest within-chromosome smoothing affects visualization only. SNV density uses a linear display; SV and TEV use log1p displays, in sites per million reference bases. TEV retains INS/DEL and simple/low-complexity/satellite repeats under the original extended definition. Small chromosome display angles are enlarged. Coral ideogram blocks denote approximate GGswu-transferred centromere candidates, including flank-only candidates; block widths are schematic, and unsupported chromosomes remain unmarked. Assembly-derived discovery is not equivalent to population allele frequency or jointly filtered genotype density. Z/W discovery provenance and centromere limitations are recorded with the data.
