# 图注 / Captions

## 中文

**图 X　环境变量对鸡 TE 变异的贡献**　

**A**：28 个候选气候变量对 TE 变异的贡献份额。逐位点（15,620 个多态 TE 位点）与每个变量做
Spearman 相关，变量内 Benjamini–Hochberg 校正后取 q<0.05，贡献份额 = 该变量的显著位点数 /
全部变量显著关联总数（n=3,940）×100。红色虚线为 1% 筛选阈值，11 个变量越过该线；横轴标签
加粗者为进一步进入面板 B 的 10 个核心变量。**B**：偏冗余分析（partial RDA，n=29 个品种；
以经纬度与 TE-PAV 矩阵前 3 个主成分作为条件变量）中 10 个核心变量的相对贡献（柱，左轴，
Shapley/LMG 分解，合计等于全模型 R²=0.495，置换检验 P=0.001）与置换重要性（折线，右轴，
999 次变量内随机置换后解释率的平均下降，归一化至 100%）。柱按置换重要性降序排列，颜色表示
变量类别。柱高接近相等反映 10 个核心变量间的共线性；置换重要性与面板 A 的边际关联不一致
（如 bio2、bio19 无显著边际关联但置换重要性最高），二者应结合解读，不能单独作为变量重要性
的排序依据。变量缩写：bio1–bio19 为 WorldClim 生物气候变量，elev 海拔，srad 太阳辐射，
wind 风速，vapr 水汽压，rh/rh_wet/rh_dry 相对湿度（年均/最湿季/最干季），dew 露点温度，
dm de Martonne 干燥指数。

## English

**Figure X. Contributions of environmental variables to TE variation in chickens.**

**A**, Contribution share of each of the 28 candidate climate variables. Every polymorphic
TE locus (n = 15,620) was correlated with each variable (Spearman), and P values were
Benjamini–Hochberg corrected within each variable (q < 0.05). The contribution share is the
number of significant loci for that variable divided by the total number of significant
associations across all variables (n = 3,940), ×100. The dashed red line marks the 1%
screening threshold; 11 variables pass it. Bold axis labels mark the 10 core variables carried
forward to **B**. **B**, Relative contributions (bars, left axis; Shapley/LMG decomposition of
the constrained variance, summing to the full-model R² = 0.495, permutation test P = 0.001) and
permutation importance (line, right axis; mean decrease in explained variance after 999
within-variable permutations, normalised to 100%) of the 10 core variables in a partial RDA
(n = 29 breeds, conditioned on longitude/latitude and the first three principal components of
the TE-PAV matrix). Bars are ordered by permutation importance; colours denote variable class.
Near-equal bar heights reflect collinearity among the 10 core variables, and permutation
importance can disagree with the marginal associations in A (e.g. bio2 and bio19 show no
significant marginal association yet the highest permutation importance); the two panels should
be interpreted jointly and neither should be used alone to rank variable importance.
